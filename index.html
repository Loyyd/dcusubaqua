About the Club

Diving, hockey, and a genuinely welcoming crew.

Since 2001

Who we are

DCU Sub-Aqua Club has been diving since 2001 and has grown into a social, adventurous group of students who train together, travel together, and spend as much time in the water as possible.

Equal parts sport, training, and social chaos in the best way.

Training Pathway

How we train

Our diving programme follows Diving Ireland and CMAS standards, with pool-based skill development leading into open-water progression when members are ready.

Why It Sticks

What makes it fun

There is room here for complete beginners, certified divers, and competitive underwater hockey players. The club works best when everyone brings energy, curiosity, and a little appetite for the unknown.

You can show up new, nervous, competitive, social, or all four at once.

New To Diving?

Start in the pool, build confidence, and work toward open water.

Beginner-friendly

No prior experience needed

If you are curious about scuba but have never tried it, the club gives you a safe on-ramp. We begin with pool sessions, equipment basics, and buoyancy drills before moving toward deeper skills.

What to expect

  • Intro sessions focused on safety and comfort in the water
  • Qualified instruction with a clear progression path
  • Pool training before any open-water diving
  • Support from experienced members and buddies

Underwater Hockey

Fast, chaotic, addictive, and much more tactical than people expect.

A second way into the water

Underwater hockey is one of the club’s standout activities. It is a team sport played on the bottom of the pool with short sticks, a puck, fins, mask, and snorkel. Sessions are intense, social, and surprisingly technical.

Why members love it

It builds breath control, awareness, movement underwater, and teamwork. It is also one of the quickest ways for new members to get involved, meet people, and start showing up regularly.

FAQ

Common questions, answered clearly.

Do I need my own gear?

Not at the beginning. New members can start with the basics and learn what equipment matters before investing in personal kit.

Can complete beginners join?

Yes. The club is built to support students who are totally new to diving as well as members who already have certifications or hockey experience.

Is it just scuba?

No. Underwater hockey, pool sessions, social events, and trips are all part of the experience, so there are multiple ways to get involved.
